Together, these technologies help maritime professionals understand vessel position, monitor surrounding conditions, plan routes, and make informed navigation decisions.

An Important Engineering Distinction

Satellite navigation systems such as GPS and Galileo provide positioning signals to receivers within their coverage conditions. However, satellite signals do not normally penetrate seawater sufficiently for direct underwater positioning. Submerged vehicles therefore rely on other technologies such as inertial navigation, Doppler Velocity Logs, and acoustic positioning systems.
