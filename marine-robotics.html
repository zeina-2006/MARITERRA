Introduction

Engineering Intelligent Systems for the Ocean

Marine robotics is an interdisciplinary field that combines mechanical engineering, electrical and electronic systems, control engineering, software development, sensing technologies, and ocean science.

Marine robots are designed to perform tasks such as underwater exploration, seabed mapping, scientific measurements, environmental monitoring, infrastructure inspection, and offshore operations. Their design depends on the environment, mission requirements, depth, operating duration, communication method, and required level of autonomy.

These systems include underwater vehicles, surface platforms, and specialized robotic equipment. Each type has different engineering requirements and operational capabilities.
